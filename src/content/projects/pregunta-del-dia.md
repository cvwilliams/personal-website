A small app I built to practice Spanish. Each day you draw the top card from a deck, and it flips
over to show a conversation question to answer out loud, write about or bring to your next language
exchange. If you get stuck, every card has an English translation, a few useful words and a
sentence starter.

- **The same card for everyone each day.** Every question comes up once before the deck reshuffles.
- **Remembers your draw** until midnight and keeps track of your streak, all in your browser, with
  no account.
- **Listen** to the question read aloud with your browser's Spanish voice.
- **Install it** on your phone's home screen, and it works offline.
- 43 questions from beginner to advanced (A1–C1).

The deck is also a self-contained Preact component, so it can drop into another site, like this one.
